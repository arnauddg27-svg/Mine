# Background music

Drop royalty-free tracks here (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`). One is picked at random per video and mixed under the voice at `music.volume` (default 12%).

Only use music you are licensed to publish on YouTube, for example tracks from the YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, or a paid library. Content ID claims on Shorts are automatic and will demonetize or mute the video.

If this folder is empty the video is rendered with voice only.
